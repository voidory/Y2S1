## 1. What the finals deck declares [source]

- **It covers Chapters 6-9 only** (the Hiang Kwee half, Q3 + Q4 = 50 marks). There is no revision deck for the Kinjo half (Q1 + Q2 = 50 marks) in what I was given.
- **It is the mid-term revision deck with parts removed and parts added.** Slides 2-32 repeat the mid-term deck (complex types, CN, oxidation state, dⁿ, denticity, nomenclature, CFT, high/low spin, CFSE, tetrahedral, LFT, π-bonding, CO back-bonding, Jahn-Teller). **Isomerism (12 slides) and complex stability (4 slides) are dropped.** That agrees with the three finals (zero marks for either) and with the 2025-26 paper saying isomerism may be ignored: treat "dropped from the finals deck" as an exam-scope signal.
- **Added (slides 33-43):** magnetism and the Guoy balance (χ, μ = 2.828 √(χT), μs = √(n(n+2)), quenching), spectroscopic terms (microstates, d¹ and d² tables, ground-term rule, predicted against observed order, term splitting in Oh, what T/E/A mean), Tanabe-Sugano (ground state on the axis, non-crossing rule, Δ, B, E), selection rules and intensity table, charge-transfer bands.

## 5. Gap list for Phase B (ranked by marks at risk times how directly the deck states it)

1. **Oxidation states of drawn organometallic and bridged complexes** (the deck's three practice items plus transfer cases: M-M bond adds nothing, alkyl counts −1, μ-OH⁻ counts −1, neutral ligands 0). About 8 questions.
2. **Oxo-anion chains** ([MnO₄]⁻, [CrO₄]²⁻, [MnO₄]²⁻, [VO₄]³⁻, [FeO₄]²⁻: name, oxidation state, dⁿ, tetrahedral splitting, CFSE, why the colour is charge transfer). The LF engine has no oxo ligand, so these are hand-authored and recomputed. About 5 questions.
3. **Magnetism chain**: χ to μ = 2.828 √(χT), then n unpaired, then high or low spin; Guoy balance reading (paramagnetic gains weight, diamagnetic loses); μs table. About 6 questions.
4. **Oh against Td CFSE ratio and Td spin-state questions** (the 27/8 mid-term item type). About 3 questions.
5. **Terms and TS concepts**: predicted against observed order of d² terms, T/E/A from occupation, non-crossing rule, what B is. About 6 questions.
6. **Selection rules, intensity ranking and charge transfer**: rank [Mn(H₂O)₆]²⁺, [Ti(H₂O)₆]³⁺, [NiCl₄]²⁻, [MnO₄]⁻ by ε; LMCT against MLCT from the oxidation state and ligand. About 5 questions.
7. **Jahn-Teller written and table variants**: strength for a list of ions, why Ti³⁺ shows two bands, consequences of elongation. About 4 questions.
8. **Changed-geometry orbital questions** (square pyramidal, square planar, tetrahedral): the mid-terms asked this in 4 of 4 years. About 3 questions.

Total about 40. Cut line if time is short: 1-5 first (about 28).
